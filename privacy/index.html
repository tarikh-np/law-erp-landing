Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Tarikh ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Tarikh legal practice management platform accessible at www.tarikh.tech. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and share information when you use our platform and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, firm name, and any other information you provide during registration or profile setup.

1.2 Practice Data

As you use Tarikh, you enter data related to your legal practice, including but not limited to: matter and case details, client information, court hearing schedules, documents, billing records, and internal notes. This data belongs to you and your firm.

1.3 Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the platform, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and actions taken within the application. This data helps us improve the platform and diagnose technical issues.

1.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and analyze platform usage. You can control cookie settings through your browser, though disabling cookies may affect certain platform functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate and maintain the Tarikh platform
  • Process your transactions and send related information such as invoices and account notifications
  • Send administrative information, including updates to our terms, policies and security alerts
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve and personalize the platform based on usage patterns and feedback
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve user experience
  • Detect, prevent and address technical issues and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations under Nepalese law

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption both in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest. We implement access controls, authentication mechanisms and regular security audits to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.
