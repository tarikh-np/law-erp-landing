Core platform

Every plan comes fully equipped

No matter which tier your firm selects, you get Tarikh's complete foundation designed specifically for Nepalese courts and legal practice.

Matter & Case Register

Organize parties, advocate assignments, matter stages, and case history in one searchable place.

Bikram Samvat & Peshi Dates

Native BS calendar with hearing tracking across Supreme Court, High Courts, and District Courts.

Document Storage

Secure cloud document management with folder structures, Preeti conversion tools, and client files.

NPR Invoicing & Retainers

Fee schedules, hourly & fixed billings, tax receipts, and payment records in Nepalese Rupees.

Kathmandu-based support team available via email, phone, and WhatsApp for your practice.
