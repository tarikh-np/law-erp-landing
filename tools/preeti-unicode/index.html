Your draft stays in the tab

Conversion runs in this browser, text and .docx alike. Nothing is uploaded, logged, stored or sent to analytics, and closing the tab clears everything. Your original document is never changed.

Use the Preeti font on this device

Preeti is a legacy encoding. This page uses the Preeti font installed on your device to show Preeti text as Nepali glyphs while preserving its original key sequence for conversion. If the text still appears as Latin letters, install the Preeti font on your device. Preeti output in Word also needs that font.

What converts, what stays

  • Line breaks, blank lines, clause numbers and English words keep their place.
  • Characters Preeti has no key for (colon, hyphen, nukta) stay as typed and are listed after each conversion.
  • A .docx keeps its paragraphs, tables, lists, headers and footers. Bold, italics, sizes and alignment stay on the text they belong to.
