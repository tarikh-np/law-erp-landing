How the conversion works

Month lengths come from the published Bikram Sambat calendar table, never from a fixed day offset. Every date is checked against its own month, so an impossible date is rejected instead of rolling forward.

Copy-ready formats

The legal line follows {BS_YEAR} saal {MM} mahina {DD} gatey {N} roj. The paired format is {BS_DATE} - {AD_DATE}, always BS first in both modes.
