Free legal tools

Tools for the paperwork around the case

Two small utilities for the work that happens between hearings: fixing a document written in Preeti, and getting a court date into the right calendar. No account, no upload, no charge.

  • Everything runs in your browser
  • No sign-in
  • Nothing stored
Preeti ↔ Unicode Converter

Turn a Preeti-encoded pleading into Nepali Unicode, or go the other way. Paste the text or upload the .docx, and keep paragraphs, clause numbers and English text.

Open converter BS ↔ AD Date Converter

Type a Bikram Sambat date and read the Gregorian date, or the reverse. Shows the weekday and the day number legal documents use, ready to copy.

Open converter

Built for Nepali practice

Both tools follow the conventions Nepali courts and offices actually use, including the numbered weekday in the legal date line.

Free, and no account

These are public tools. You do not need a Tarikh login, and no part of your text or date is kept after you close the tab.
