Platform Features

Every feature your law firm needs, built into one platform

Tarikh brings matter management, court hearing tracking, client records, documents, billing and reporting into a single connected workflow designed for legal practice in Nepal.

Core Module

Matter & Case Management

Open a matter, assign parties, track progress and keep every document, hearing and billing entry connected to it. Every matter in Tarikh holds its full history — status, court details, assigned team members and milestones — so nothing falls through the cracks.

Matters can be organized by type, court, status or custom tags your firm defines. Whether you handle litigation, family law, corporate advisory or property disputes, the structure adapts to your workflow rather than forcing you into a rigid template.

  • Matter profiles with status, progress and assigned team
  • Parties linked to the right cases
  • Court, case number and hearing type for every matter
  • Timeline of key milestones and activity
  • Custom tags and filters for your firm's categories

Court Tracking

Court hearings, Tarikh/Peshi dates and deadlines

Record every court event — hearings, Tarikh/Peshi dates, mentions, filing deadlines and case conferences — and attach them directly to the relevant matter. The calendar view gives your entire firm a clear picture of what is coming up and when.

Tarikh understands Nepal's court terminology. Hearing types, court levels and case reference formats are structured to match how your firm actually tracks dates, not how a generic calendar app expects you to enter them.

  • Hearings, Tarikh/Peshi, mentions and filing deadlines
  • Court and case reference for every event
  • Court-level categorization (District, Appellate, Supreme)
  • Firm-wide calendar and upcoming events dashboard
  • Event reminders and status tracking

Client Records

Client management that keeps relationships in context

Keep every client's contact details, communication history and matter relationships in one place. When you open a client profile, you see every matter connected to them — past and present — along with related parties and key details.

This gives your team the full picture of a client relationship without searching through email threads, spreadsheets or separate contact tools.

  • Client profiles with contact details and tags
  • All matters for a client in one view
  • Parties linked to the right cases
  • Communication history and notes
  • Active and closed matter tracking

Document Management

Legal documents connected to the right matter

Upload, organize and version documents directly within each matter. Every file stays connected to the case it belongs to, with controlled access so the right team members see the right files.

From engagement letters and pleadings to court orders and correspondence, your documents are organized by matter rather than scattered across shared drives and email inboxes.

  • Upload, organize and version documents
  • Link every file to a matter or client
  • Access-controlled document management
  • Supported formats: PDF, DOC, DOCX, XLS, XLSX and more
  • Full-text search across your document library

Billing & Invoicing

Billing and invoices without the busywork

Track time and expenses against individual matters, generate professional invoices and monitor payment status — all without leaving your workflow. Every billing entry connects back to the matter it belongs to.

See outstanding balances, payment history and revenue across your firm in one view, so your billing stays organized and your cash flow stays visible.

  • Time and expense tracking per matter
  • Invoice generation with clear status
  • Outstanding balances at a glance
  • Payment history and collections tracking
  • Firm-wide billing and revenue reports

Security & Access

Role-based access and firm-wide security

Tarikh includes role-based access with configurable permissions, so each team member sees and manages only what their role allows. Partners, associates, paralegals and administrative staff each get the access level that fits their work.

Every important action across the system is recorded in audit logs, giving your firm visibility into what happened, when and by whom.

  • Role-based access with configurable permissions
  • Granular control over what users can view and edit
  • Audit logs for firm-wide activity tracking
  • Secure data storage with encryption in transit and at rest
  • Team and user management for your firm's structure
