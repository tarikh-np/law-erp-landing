Pricing

Simple pricing for your firm

Tarikh offers flexible plans designed to match your firm's size and needs. Every plan includes core case management features, secure hosting and dedicated support.

Get a plan that fits your firm

Every law firm is different. Whether you are a solo practitioner, a small firm or a growing practice, we will work with you to find the right plan. Contact us for a tailored quote and to discuss which features suit your workflow.
